Who Comes to School Fairs?

Different types of customers look for different things:

Other Kids

What they want: Fun, cool, trendy items

What they care about: Will my friends think this is awesome?

Parents

What they want: Quality items, supporting kids

What they care about: Is this worth the money? Will it last?

Teachers

What they want: Educational or classroom supplies

What they care about: Can I use this for teaching?

Grandparents

What they want: Something special from their grandkid's school

What they care about: This will make a great memory!

Offline Marketing Methods

You don't need the internet to spread the word!

4 Great Ways to Market at School Fairs

Visual Marketing

Catch people's attention with what they can see

  • Colorful signs and banners
  • Eye-catching product displays
  • Before/after photos of your crafts
  • Bright tablecloths and decorations

Word-of-Mouth Marketing

Let happy customers spread the word for you

  • Friends telling other friends
  • Family spreading the word
  • Happy customers coming back
  • Asking customers to tell others

Sample Marketing

Let customers try before they buy

  • Free tastes of food products
  • Touch and feel crafts
  • Live demonstrations
  • Show how products work

Story Marketing

Share the story behind your business

  • Why you started your business
  • How you make your product
  • What makes it special
  • Fun facts about your process

Create Your Marketing Message

The Simple Message Formula

A great marketing message has 4 parts:

1

What

What you're selling (in 3 words or less)

"Friendship bracelets"
2

Why

What makes it special (1 unique thing)

"You can customize them"
3

Who

Who it's perfect for

"Perfect for best friends"
4

Call to Action

What you want them to do

"Come pick your colors!"
