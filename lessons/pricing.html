How to Price Your Product

Let's learn how to set the perfect price for your business!

Why is Pricing Important?

Setting the right price is super important for your business because:

  • You need to make money to keep your business running
  • If the price is too high, people won't buy
  • If the price is too low, you won't make enough money
  • The right price makes both you and your customers happy!

Understanding Your Costs

Before setting a price, you need to know what things cost to make!

What Goes Into Your Costs?

Materials

Everything you need to make your product (fabric, wood, ingredients, etc.)

Time

How long it takes you to make the product (your time is valuable!)

Tools & Equipment

Special tools, machines, or equipment needed

Smart Pricing Strategies

Now that you know your costs, let's learn different ways to set your price!

Different Ways to Price Your Product

Cost-Plus Pricing

Take your cost and add some extra for profit

Example: Cost $6.50 + Profit $3.50 = $10.00

Market Research

Look at what similar products cost and price yours competitively

Example: Other bracelets sell for $8-12, so price yours at $9.00

Value-Based Pricing

Price based on how special or unique your product is

Example: Custom name bracelets are special, so charge $15.00

Smart Pricing Tips:

Always cover your costs first
Research what competitors charge
Consider what makes your product special
Don't be afraid to adjust prices if needed
